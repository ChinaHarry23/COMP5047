# EDGE weather sky notes

## Future improvement: photoreal lightning (not implemented)
- Now: scripted double-flicker on a separate "Lightning Flash" directional light + procedural thunder rumble (EdgeWeatherSky.cs).
- Upgrade path: procedural branching bolt mesh or a VFX Graph strike, with an HDRP volumetric light flash at the strike point; lightning-lit HDRP Volumetric Clouds (pulse cloud lighting during the flash).
- Asset Store lightning/storm packs are an alternative (check HDRP compatibility and licence).
- Crest is an ocean/water rendering plugin. It helps only with wet surfaces or puddles, not lightning.
